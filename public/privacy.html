Privacy Policy

This application accesses bank account data solely for Marv's internal use, to view balances and transactions across its own business bank accounts.

No account data is shared with, sold to, or accessed by any third party. Data is used exclusively to power Marv's internal banking dashboard.

Consent

Connecting a bank account requires logging into that bank directly and explicitly authorizing data access through the bank's own Open Banking consent screen (via Plaid). No account is connected without this direct authorization from the account holder.

Data we store

We store the access credential issued by your bank's Open Banking connection and your recent transaction history, encrypted at rest. We do not store your bank login username or password — those are entered directly with your bank, never with this application.

Retention and deletion

Transaction history is limited to the 200 most recent transactions per connected account. Disconnecting a bank account (via the "Disconnect" option) immediately and permanently deletes its stored data, including the access credential, from our systems.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the person responsible for information security at Marv.
